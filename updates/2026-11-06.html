Valuing heart over head

Being sick gave me some time to reflect this week.

I realized that for GoodApp Labs to be wildly successful, I have to value heart over head: to value the joy of creating, having fun, exploring and taking risky bets. Even if that sometimes means choosing what feels alive over what might seem like a surer thing financially.

This means going against what I said last week and continuing to iterate on Dream in a Jar, since I find joy and feel connected to the project even though the back-of-the-envelope math doesn't point to a huge monetization opportunity. It also means putting my marketing effort into what feels energizing and authentic, rather than what I think I should do because it might perform better.

It also got me thinking about our mission. I realized that solving problems creates working products, but bringing dreams to life is what creates joy and love. So there you go, our new working mission: bring dreams to life. 🫙✨
