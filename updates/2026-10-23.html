Where AI shines, and where it struggles

We're starting to see which parts of building a product AI can handle on its own. Overall, it feels pretty good: big parts of the build can be handed off to agents. The big unknown is automating marketing. I think that's what will really make or break the business model.

Here's how much of each stage AI can handle today:

  1. Scouting100%

    AI collects raw ideas and feedback from forums and app stores completely on its own.

  2. Inventing ideas66%

    Still a struggle. AI gets close on the broader space, but picking out the truly insightful, magical opportunity is hard. I'm optimistic more tuning will get us there.

  3. Research95%

    AI mapped user journeys surprisingly well, better than I could have on my own. Light feedback takes care of minor issues.

  4. Design50%

    AI can't build good wireframes on its own and needs a lot of input. Luckily, we're good at this and enjoy it, and AI makes it much faster, more iterative and more fun.

  5. Build75%

    Agents do basically all the building, but higher-level technical decisions need my testing and input. I hope to automate more with product manager agents that oversee the builders, though it hasn't slowed us down much so far.

  6. Marketing & iterationTBD

    Not started yet, since nothing has launched. This is the big one.
