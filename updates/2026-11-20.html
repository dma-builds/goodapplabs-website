A new project: The Ivy Quiz

I started working on The Ivy Quiz, an app that helps A students get a sense of how they'd rate in a mock college interview, and what they can do to increase their odds of getting into an Ivy League–tier school.

Dream in a Jar: clinking dreams

I'm working on a feature where parents and kids can clink their phones together to make shared dreams. It's not in TestFlight yet, but it's coming out super cute. 🫙🥂🫙 The hope is that it encourages people to share and use the app in person.

Unlocks on marketing

There have been several here.

1. Marketing as a way to serve

I asked myself why it's been so hard to get to marketing, and found myself reading This is Marketing by Seth Godin. It has several life-altering philosophies that helped me reframe marketing from “advertising” to a way to serve others and change the world.

2. I was wrong about Fable

I initially thought Claude Fable wouldn't do well at things that require human judgment, like marketing campaigns and design, because I thought thinking was the enemy of that kind of work. I also ignored it because I didn't want to spend down my tokens so fast. But I was wrong.

I once had Claude Opus write up a marketing strategy doc, and it just wasn't good. This time I asked Fable to write one using the teachings of This is Marketing. I gotta say, it's compelling, and it makes me excited to work on marketing for Dream in a Jar.

3. Fewer apps, higher bar

I realized marketing is going to take a lot more time than I thought, so I might need more time with each project. I'm updating our goals: cutting my ship target in half, and aiming for a higher success rate.
