Dream in a Jar

Every dream your child says out loud, painted and kept.

Your child says a dream out loud. Dream in a Jar turns it into words, paints a picture of it, and seals it in a jar on a little shelf, so your family can look back on everything they once hoped for.

Made for parents to use alongside their children, at bedtime or any time a dream comes up.
