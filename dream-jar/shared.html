Dream Jar

Someone sent you a dream.

A child said it out loud, and Dream Jar painted it. Each dream is sealed in a jar on a little shelf, so a family can look back on everything their kid hoped for.

Made for parents to use with their children, at bedtime or any time a dream comes up.
